PRIVACY POLICY

WallFall / DOE Studio privacy information. This page is structured for the game's current services and will be updated as WallFall evolves.

1. Who this policy is for

This Privacy Policy describes how DOE Studio handles information associated with WallFall, its competitive multiplayer barricade game, and the connected services used by the game.

2. Information used by WallFall

Based on the current product configuration, WallFall uses account and player information, guest account information, player IDs, cloud player profile information, device-related information where applicable, notification-related information, reports and moderation information, and information needed to operate account linking and deletion.

  • Account type and account-related identifiers.
  • Player ID and cloud profile information.
  • Device-related information where applicable to the game's operation or connected services.
  • Information associated with player reports, blocking, and moderation actions.
  • Notification-related information for Firebase Cloud Messaging.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to provide accounts, cloud profiles, multiplayer features, player interactions, moderation and reporting, blocking, notifications, account linking, account deletion, and the operation of the game's connected services.

4. Service providers

WallFall currently uses the following services as part of the game:

  • Nakama for backend / multiplayer services.
  • Google Play Games Services for Google Play game-account functionality.
  • Firebase / Firebase Cloud Messaging for Firebase-connected functions and push notifications.
  • AdMob for advertising, including rewarded and interstitial advertisements.

5. Advertising

WallFall uses AdMob and may display rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements. Additional provider-specific privacy information may apply to advertising activity.

6. Notifications

Firebase Cloud Messaging is used for notifications. The information required by that service may be processed by Firebase according to its own policies and configuration.

7. Reporting, blocking, and moderation

WallFall includes player reporting and blocking. Reports may contain information associated with the reported player and the selected report reason. Moderation actions may use this information to support game administration and player safety.

8. Data retention

Retention periods depend on the type of data and the service involved. This page does not claim a specific retention period unless DOE Studio has formally confirmed one.

9. Account deletion

Players can permanently delete their WallFall account from inside the game's account/settings section. See the dedicated Account Deletion page for the in-game flow.

10. Security

DOE Studio does not make a blanket security certification or encryption claim on this page. Specific technical safeguards should be added only after they are formally confirmed.

11. Children's privacy

The game involves account, advertising, notification, and player-interaction systems. DOE Studio applies the age and privacy controls required by the services and distribution platforms it uses.

12. Policy changes

This policy may be updated as WallFall and DOE Studio's services change. When material changes are made, the published policy will be updated accordingly.

13. Contact

Privacy questions: support.doe.studio@gmail.com
