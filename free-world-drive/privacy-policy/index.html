FREE WORLD DRIVE
PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy for Free World Drive: Car Racing, a driving game by DOE Studio. This game is built to work with no account and no sign-in of any kind.

In short: Free World Drive: Car Racing does not use sign-in, login, or accounts of any kind. There is no server-side account tied to you. All game progress and settings are saved locally on your own device.

1. No accounts, no sign-in

Free World Drive: Car Racing does not require or offer any sign-in, login, guest account, or social/account linking (no email login, no Google/Play Games sign-in, no third-party authentication). You can play the full game without ever creating an identity tied to you.

2. Where your data is stored

Game progress, settings, unlocked cars, and any other save data are stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. This data is never uploaded to DOE Studio's servers, because DOE Studio does not operate a backend for this game.

3. Information we collect

DOE Studio does not collect, transmit, or have access to any personal information from Free World Drive: Car Racing. We do not receive your save data, device identifiers, location, contacts, or any other personal information from the game.

4. Third-party services

Free World Drive: Car Racing does not integrate any account, analytics, advertising, or cloud-save service. If a future update introduces a third-party service (for example, advertising or analytics), this policy will be updated in advance of that change to disclose exactly what is collected and why.

5. Children's privacy

Because the game collects no personal information and requires no account, no personal information is knowingly collected from children or any other player.

6. Uninstalling the game

Uninstalling Free World Drive: Car Racing removes its locally stored save data from your device, following your device's normal app-removal behavior. Since no copy of your data exists on our servers, there is nothing further for us to delete.

7. Changes to this policy

If Free World Drive: Car Racing's data practices ever change (for example, if an account system, cloud save, or advertising is added), this page will be updated first, and the update will clearly describe the change.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: support.doe.studio@gmail.com
