ACCOUNT CONTROL

DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

Permanent WallFall / DOE Studio account deletion is available from inside the game.

Permanent action: Account deletion is intended to be permanent. Make sure you are certain before confirming.

How to delete your WallFall account

  1. Open WallFall.
  2. Open the Settings / Account section.
  3. Use the Delete Account control.
  4. Complete the in-game confirmation flow.

What happens next

The game provides an account deletion control for account-associated data. Some data may be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or service-operation reasons where required.

Cannot access the game?

Use the fallback contact method below and provide enough information for the support team to identify the request.
