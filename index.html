What is WallFall

BLUE VS RED. LAST WALL STANDING.

A fast, tactical 1v1 board game: place walls, box in your opponent, and win the match before they box in you.

HOW TO PLAY

Pick a mode, drop your walls, don't get boxed in.

Jump into Ranked for the leaderboard, Casual for a quick match, Play with Friend for a private lobby, or Practice with Bot to learn the ropes — all from one clean home screen.

SOCIAL LAYER

Players have identities beyond the board.

Player IDs, profiles, friends, account types, reporting, blocking, notifications, and account deletion are all part of the live product.
