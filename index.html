PLAYER SYSTEMS

WallFall isn't only a board. The product includes the surrounding account, social, and moderation systems that make a live multiplayer game usable.

01

Accounts & profiles

Guest accounts, player IDs, cloud player profiles, and Google Play Games integration are part of the current product direction.

02

03

Reporting categories, blocking, moderation flows, and account-level controls are treated as first-class UI.
